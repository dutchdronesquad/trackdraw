# Race Line ground marker preview

Issue [#1068](https://github.com/dutchdronesquad/trackdraw/issues/1068) adds optional planning dots to the TrackDraw Studio and read-only 3D Viewer. Open either 3D view and pick a Race Line in **Ground markers** after reviewing the route. Choose **Off** to hide the dots independently of the floating line. The choice applies to the current 3D view and design; it starts off and has no project or share setting. Missing routes produce no overlay and never cause another route to be chosen automatically.

Dots use the ground distance along the same curve as the floating line. They start at approximately four-metre intervals, spread out to cover long routes with up to 96 candidates, avoid the first and last two metres of open routes, and wrap the seam on closed routes. Short runs may have no dots. Gaps preserve equipment and opening clearance, field edges, high or steep sections and places where another part of the route passes nearby. A simple close bend follows the curve; a tight hairpin, repeat or crossing with competing passes can leave a gap. These rules conservatively omit spots without deciding which opening, layer or pass is intended. Flat dots cannot depict a split-S, powerloop or stacked passage.

The same dots appear in the interactive fly-through and in a PNG taken from the current opaque 3D view. Overview and transparent PNGs, rendered fly-through video, other project/geometry exports, standalone embeds using the separate Viewer package, and future time-based race replay do not read the local view choice. Keep dots out of selection, numbering, undo, inventory, estimates and equipment counts.

For Race Pack follow-up, try a separate, opt-in briefing diagram that pairs a reviewed route and its gaps with the existing measurements (#1038). Dots can help crew visualize broad course flow, but gaps cannot specify turns or exact equipment positions. Do not include preview counts in the bill of materials; field crew need to verify each real placement. Keep conversion into cone or marker equipment out of this slice. If added later, make it an explicit reviewed batch edit with a single undo step and real inventory quantities.
